## Video direction

- **palette system** (frame.md, Kage light): ground bg #FBFAF9 on every frame (flat; one faint primary-tinted radial wash allowed behind the focal on frames 01, 04, 06); display + body text #0E100F; secondary lines text-muted #45404E; chrome/kickers text-light #6B6579; the ONE accent primary #5B41E0 on exactly one word/glyph per frame; negative #A0055D only for the BOT dots/tags in 02; positive #0B7D56 only for the "Validated by Kuru" pill in 05. Type by role: display Geist 800 tight (about -0.05em), chrome Geist Mono caps (0.14em), the 影 mark from KageMark (Noto Serif JP glyph) with a hard purple offset shadow.
- **motion grammar + reveal model**: long-tail eases (power3 / expo.out default, smooth over bouncy; one spring-pop allowed on each frame's payoff). Silent film, so every reveal is cued to its on-screen text beat; read order = reveal order: kicker/chrome first, then the headline line by line, then the payoff. Never front-load; each frame spends its back half resolving the last beat, then holds.
- **rhythm / holds**: 01 builds fast (count-up), 02 builds in sequence, 03 is the turn (fastest swap, then a held read on the 影 line), 04-05 are product proof (phones arrive, then hold still to be read), 06 is the held lockup (stillest frame, longest hold). Holds keep at most a subtle drift on phones (1% scale or less), no breathing on type.
- **chrome**: every frame except 06 carries the mono kicker top-left and the frame number top-right, plus a mono footnote bottom-left on 01/02 (source/receipt). Content stays in the top ~83%.
- **negative list**: no dark grounds; no gradients on type; no stock shapes, bokeh or "AI" purple-blue gradients; no second accent colour beyond the semantic bot/check tags; no invented numbers (only 61,894 / 5 / 4 / 1 / ~90 and what the real screenshots show); no Nansen, volume or user claims; no slideshow (front-load then freeze) and no screensaver (everything floating independently).
